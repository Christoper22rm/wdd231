About Me

Hello, world! I’m Christoper Calvachi, CEO of Brailix and currently a student at BYU. I love programming and providing solid, practical solutions to people, especially businesses looking to improve through artificial intelligence and software. My motto is: If you can dream it, you can achieve it. I studied computer science in high school; I’ve known how to program since I was 13. I used to do it at an internet café, paying 30 cents for half an hour. I served a mission in Bahía Blanca, Argentina; after returning from it, during the pandemic, I spent my time at home programming projects, including some to illuminate the world and some for local events near my community. I took programming courses at Harvard, where I specialized further in artificial intelligence; then I took on the challenge of studying programming at BYU, which has been an enriching experience in my life.
