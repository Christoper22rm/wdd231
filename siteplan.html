Electric Vehicles Hub - Site Plan

Site Name

Electric Vehicles Hub

This name represents a centralized platform focused on electric vehicles, providing users with relevant information, comparisons, and resources about EV technology.

Optional Domain Availability: evhub.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide users with detailed information about electric vehicles, including comparisons between models, benefits of EV technology, and resources for people interested in transitioning from traditional vehicles to electric mobility.

Scenarios

  • What is the best electric car available in the market right now?
  • How much can I save by switching to an electric vehicle?
  • Where can I find charging stations near me?

Color Scheme

Blue (#0077ff) – Used for buttons, links, and highlights.

Dark Blue (#0a2540) – Used for headers and footer.

Light Gray (#f4f6f9) – Used for background.

Typography

  • Poppins – Used for headings and main content.
  • Arial – Backup font for compatibility.
